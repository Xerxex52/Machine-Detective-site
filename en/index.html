Questions

Do I need to know mechanics already?+

No. The first missions start with a wheel and a crank. Speed, force and transmission are introduced gradually, with lessons to revisit each idea.

What can I build in the workshop?+

Mechanisms with shafts, gears, motors, pulleys and loads. Starter builds include a multi-stage winch. Change the parts and save your own projects.

Does it cover 3D printing too?+

Some missions connect mechanisms to printer axes, belts and steps per millimetre. The game explains principles; it does not replace real machine specifications.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?+

After downloading, the game works offline without an account. Progress and builds stay on your device, with no cross-device sync.

What is included?+

All missions, the workshop and lessons. The planned price in France is €7.99. Prices in other countries will be shown in the App Store.
